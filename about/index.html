About Big Easy Paintings

At Big Easy Painting, we are more than just a painting company, we are your partners in transforming your spaces into works of art. With a passion for perfection and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction, we have been serving New Orleans and the surrounding areas for years.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful idea: to redefine the painting industry by combining exceptional craftsmanship, top-notch materials, and a customer-centric approach. Since our inception, we have successfully brought countless residential and commercial projects to life, earning a reputation as the go-to painting professional in New Orleans.

Interior painting

Our interior painting services are designed to breathe new life into your home or office. Whether you want a cozy atmosphere with warm colors or a modern, sleek look with neutral tones, we have the expertise to make it happen. We pay meticulous attention to detail, ensuring smooth finishes on walls, ceilings, trim and more.
